Meet Horsefly the Superhero

Not all superheroes wear capes. Some have four legs, a gentle heart, and a special way of making people smile. Meet Horsefly! His mission is to spread happiness, brighten difficult days, and remind people that a little kindness can make a big difference.

Whether he is visiting a hospital, spending time with nursing home residents, or attending a community event, Horsefly is always ready to make someone's day a little brighter.
